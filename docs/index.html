papers-from-scratch

Hard research papers, implemented from scratch. No framework shortcuts — the actual mechanism (autograd, normalization, conv, attention, optimizer) is hand-rolled, not called from a library.

Every folder carries a JOURNAL.md: the paper's own quoted claims next to what actually happened implementing them — sourcing fights, transcription bugs, dead gradients, wrong hyperparameters, the fixes, and the final numbers against the paper's own.
